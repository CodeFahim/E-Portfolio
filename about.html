About me

Software Engineering student at AIUB in Dhaka, looking for a web development or QA internship from January 2027.

I got into software through competitive programming, solving problems in C++ for ICPC. That taught me to think about edge cases before anything else, which turned out to be most of what testing is.

At AIUB I major in Software Engineering. For a team project (CSC 4182) I built the admin module of PawsHome BD, a NestJS backend for an animal adoption platform. Debugging its auth guard, email delivery and database constraints taught me more than any lecture.

This semester I’m taking Software Quality and Testing and Software Requirements Engineering. Together they changed how I build: I write down what “working” means first, then prove it with tests.

Outside coursework I do machine learning research on explainability and calibration, with papers accepted at IEEE conferences in 2026.

I’m looking for a team where I can ship real features, get my code reviewed, and learn how professionals test at scale.
